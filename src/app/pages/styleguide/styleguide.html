TechTags

Logos from Simple Icons in the accent colour; a tech without a logo gets a › mark. Used in the hero and in case-study Stack sections. The journey timeline is on the homepage.

SectionStepper and Reveal

Page-level: on the homepage, wide screens show the section stepper on the right edge, and content below the fold fades in as it scrolls into view (never with reduced motion).

ThemeToggle

Switches between dark and light; the new theme spreads from the button as a wave (instant with reduced motion). The choice is remembered; until then the system setting applies.

SoundToggle

Off by default. When on, links, buttons and tabs tick; the theme toggle plays a wave and a sent message a short chime. data-sound="off" silences an element.

BackToTop

Site-wide: scroll down more than a screen on this page and the round button appears at the bottom right. It stays above the footer and moves focus to the logo.
